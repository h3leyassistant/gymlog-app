# Assets

## Anatomy

- File: `assets/anatomy-atlas.png`
- Created on 2026-09-28 with the built-in image_gen tool (not CLI).
- Generated for this GymLog brainstorming/prototype task; copied into this project from the Codex generated_images directory.
- Synthetic training mannequin, front/back concept illustration. It is not a licensed third-party 3D model and is not a clinically validated anatomy dataset.
- `assets/anatomy-cutout.webp` is this image with the background made transparent; `js/anatomy-regions.js` holds muscle outlines traced from its grooves. Both are generated by `tools/segment_anatomy.py`; no pixels of the figure were repainted.
- Each view is clipped in SVG.

### Final prompt

```text
A premium fitness educational illustration on solid dark charcoal #101114 background, landscape 3:2 aspect ratio. Exactly TWO full length graphite gray athletic training mannequins wearing opaque black mid-thigh sports shorts, side by side with plenty of margin. One front view on left, one back view on right, perfectly straight orthographic views, identical height and pose. A synthetic anatomical teaching sculpture like a gym museum display, with muscles sculpted into the gray resin surface to show muscle groups, not a human photograph. Both mannequins wear black opaque workout shorts fully covering waist, hips and upper thighs. Hands at sides slightly away from torso. Full figure from head at 6% to feet at 94% image height, centers at 25% and 75% image width. Strong realistic athletic proportions. Visible sculptural pectorals, deltoids, arm muscle groups, abdominal segments, back and calf muscle forms with fine sculpted striations. Beautiful silver studio key light, dark gray shaded contours, refined volumetric 3D rendering. No colored highlights. Synthetic faceless head. Uniform flat #101114 background, no floor, shadows, text, labels, icons or interface. Perfectly matched figure size and alignment. This is a clean two-view educational fitness mannequin asset for an app.
```

## Exercise photos

- Files: `assets/exercises/<exercise-id>-0.webp` (start) and `-1.webp` (end), 33 exercises. `assets/exercises/sources.json` maps each mock exercise id to its source exercise.
- Source: [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), downloaded 2026-09-29, released under the Unlicense. Resized to 480px wide and converted to WebP; no other edits.
- The repository says the photos came from the earlier exercises.json project; their original chain of rights is not documented. Fine for this mock, but confirm the rights or replace the photos before a commercial release.
- Three photos show a close variant: Goblet Squat (kettlebell), Machine Hip Thrust (barbell), Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift (stiff-legged dumbbell deadlift). The detail dialog says so.
- 4-Way Neck Machine and Cable Hip Abduction have no match and keep the authored sketch.
